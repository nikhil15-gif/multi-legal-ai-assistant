RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT is made on this 1st day of August, 2024 at Bengaluru, Karnataka.

BETWEEN:
Mr. Ramesh Kumar, residing at #14, Indiranagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "LESSOR / LANDLORD") of the FIRST PART;
AND
Ms. Priya Sharma, residing at #42, Koramangala, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "LESSEE / TENANT") of the SECOND PART.

1. DEMISE AND TERM:
The Landlord hereby leases the residential premises situated at Flat 302, Green Meadows, Bengaluru for a term of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st August, 2024.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND DEPOSIT:
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 28,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of every calendar month. An interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/- has been deposited with the Landlord.

3. NOTICE AND TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing one calendar month written notice or rent in lieu thereof. The Landlord shall not abruptly disconnect water or electricity supply without due process of law.

4. INDEMNITY AND REPAIRS:
The Tenant agrees to indemnify the Landlord against any damage caused to fixtures through negligence. Day-to-day minor repairs up to Rs. 1,000/- shall be borne by the Tenant, while structural repairs shall be the sole responsibility of the Landlord.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND JURISDICTION:
Any dispute arising under this agreement shall be settled through mutual discussion, failing which the civil courts in Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties hereto have signed this deed on the day, month and year first above written.
